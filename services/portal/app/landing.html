What is OxBlood?

OxBlood represents the intensity and precision required in modern cyber operations. Our platform embodies three core principles:

Operational Excellence - Every exercise is designed to simulate real-world adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs). We don't just teach theory; we immerse you in realistic scenarios that mirror actual threat landscapes.

Benchmarking Standards - Measure and improve your defensive capabilities against known threats. Our platform provides objective metrics to track your team's progress and identify areas for improvement.

Live Operations - Gain hands-on experience with actual offensive and defensive scenarios. Deploy real infrastructure, execute real attacks, and defend against real threats in controlled environments.

Our mission is to prepare cyber teams for the realities of modern threats through realistic, high-intensity training exercises that build muscle memory and operational readiness.
